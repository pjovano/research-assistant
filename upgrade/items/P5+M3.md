## Builds

| Piece | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `Scheduler` | `install(job)`, `installed(job)`, `remove(job)`; a job is a label and its contents; one adapter, launchd |
| launchd adapter | builds a plist from a job's label and contents, hard-coded today at `lib/_health/installer.py:40` and `lib/_health/launch_agent.py:32-42`; `install` with `launchctl bootstrap`; `remove` with `launchctl bootout` |
| Health | the health job takes a new label; `health.py uninstall [--apply]`, read-only form first |
| Hook | `HealthWatcherHook` in `lib/harness_hooks.py` installs through `Scheduler` |
| Remote Control job | `caffeinate -i claude remote-control --spawn same-dir`, `KeepAlive`, working directory `~/Developer/agent-core`, a label other than `local.claude-remote-control`, no `--name`; keeps the hand-written plist's other keys (`RunAtLoad`, `ThrottleInterval` 30, `--no-create-session-in-dir`, a `PATH` reaching `claude` and Homebrew, a log under `~/Library/Logs/`); resolves `claude` at install time; `install [--apply]` and `uninstall [--apply]`, read-only form first |
| Skill | `machine-health`: `uninstall` |

Not built: the systemd adapter, `SystemReadings` and the `Level` scale, and
ntfy. The readings and the detectors stay as they are.

Code only: the jobs are installed at the switch (§6 step 4.6), never before.
The hand-written plists are NEVER read, edited, or removed by this code: the
rollback reloads them.
